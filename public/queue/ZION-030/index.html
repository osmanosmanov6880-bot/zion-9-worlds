Field evidence · not game scoring

A 2025 Athens field report described surface readings around 60°C on exposed paving and 30°C under tree shade at Argentinis Dimokratias Square. The 3D puzzle uses that finding as context; its points are illustrative, not scientific predictions.

Technology already in use

Athens combines shaded/green spaces with a Heat Risk Index and the #CoolAthens information service. Explore the full field episode, sources and business model ↗.
